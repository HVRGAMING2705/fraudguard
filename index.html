🛡 FraudGuard — Card Fraud Detector

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A logistic regression model trained on 1,000 synthetic card transactions scores fraud risk from amount, hour, distance from home, recent transactions, foreign flag and card-present flag. 5-fold CV: accuracy = 75.7%, AUC = 0.77 vs an always-legit baseline of 71.2%.

How it works: the inputs are converted to model features (log amount, night-time flag, log distance, capped recent-transaction count, foreign and card-present flags), standardised with the training means and standard deviations, then multiplied by the model's learned coefficients and passed through a sigmoid. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try a sample: a normal daytime purchase — ₹2,500 at 2 PM, 5 km from home, card present → about 15.4% fraud risk. A risky one — ₹85,000 at 3 AM, 1,200 km away, foreign, card not present → about 99.9% fraud risk.
